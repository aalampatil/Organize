# Code of Conduct

Organize welcomes contributors of every background and experience level. Help keep the project respectful, useful, and comfortable to participate in.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and assume good intent when asking questions or reviewing changes.
- Give specific, constructive feedback about the work.
- Respect different perspectives, accessibility needs, and levels of experience.
- Credit other people's work and respect their privacy.
- Keep conversations relevant to the project and use clear, professional language.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized comments, repeated unwelcome attention, impersonation, and publishing someone's private information are not acceptable. Deliberately disrupting discussions or continuing behavior after a maintainer asks you to stop is also unacceptable.

Do not post another person's notes, files, email codes, session tokens, or credentials in issues, pull requests, screenshots, or logs.

## Reporting a concern

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer at [aalam.workacc@gmail.com](mailto:aalam.workacc@gmail.com). Include links or screenshots when helpful, with unrelated personal information removed. Use the [security policy](SECURITY.md) for software vulnerabilities.

Maintainers will review reports, protect personal details where possible, and decide on an appropriate response. Responses can include a request to change behavior, a warning, content removal, temporary restrictions, or removal from project spaces. Serious abuse can result in immediate restrictions.

## Scope and responsibility

This policy applies to the repository's issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces maintained for Organize. Maintainers are responsible for applying it fairly and explaining moderation decisions when doing so does not compromise someone's privacy or safety.
